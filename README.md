# keymouse

If you can't find a mouse, the palm of your hand keep pressing the touchpad or you just prefer using the keyboard, this tool let you use w,a,s,d to move the cursor, j,k,l for left, middle and right click and y,u,i,o for scrolling left, down, up and right. Hold alt+shift ta activate keymouse and hold h for hyperspeed and p for slow speed.
